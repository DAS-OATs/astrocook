astrocook.methods_text module#

The log of a session as the text of a methods section (phase 6-ter, PR 5c).

methods_text() writes what was done to a session as English text, to paste in the methods section of a paper (decision 5 of phase 6-ter): an opening paragraph, one paragraph per recipe of the log, a closing paragraph on the fit and the atomic data, and the references.

A paragraph of a recipe is made of:

  • the reason given for the recipe (LogEntryV2.reason, e.g. by an agent through the MCP server), as written;

  • what the recipe did, from the key methods of the registry (a template of its parameters, or a function of them), with the menu of the recipe in parentheses (menus=False leaves it out), and the parameters the text does not quote that differ from their defaults;

  • what the recipe changed, from the comparison of the state before and after it (compare_sessions(), as RecipeResult does): the components added, removed and changed, the columns, the pixels; and the warnings the fit left on the components;

  • the notes written at its state (LogNoteV2: notes and gaps declared, as written; the gaps seen by the MCP server are for the development and are left out).

The recipes undone are left out. The text is made from the log each time it is asked for, never stored: a better wording reaches the sessions saved before it. Nothing here names a recipe: a recipe added to the registry with its methods is described with no code here, one without it with a generic sentence.

The parameters of a log entry, as the methods of the registry read them: p['kappa'] is the value as text (fmt()), p.raw('kappa') the value as logged, p.menu the menu of the recipe in parentheses (with a space before it) or “”. astrocook.methods_text.methods_text(source, *, menus: bool = True) → MethodsText[source]#

The log of a session as the text of a methods section.

Parameters:
