astrocook.fitting.model_evaluation module#

The evaluation of a model on a fixed set of pixels, without fitting (findings register F154, phase 7, PR 7a.5).

The chi2 of a model and its information criteria (AIC, AICc, BIC and the SpIC of Webb et al. 2021) on pixels chosen by the user, so that two alternative models can be compared on the same pixels: the basis of the comparison between alternative identifications (phase 7c) and of the evaluation of an imported model (7a.3).

The model is the whole system list; a selection of its components decides only which of them are counted: their free parameters (k) and their terms of the SpIC. Of the selection, only the components that influence the pixels are counted (MIN_INFLUENCE).

SpIC (Webb, Lee, Carswell & Milakovic 2021, MNRAS 501, 2268, doi:10.1093/mnras/staa3551, eq. 3, 5, 6):

SpIC = chi2 + sum_a [ 2 f k_a R_a / (R_a - k_a - 1) + (1 - f) k_a ln R_a ]
R_a  = sum_i (1 - I^a_i) / sigma_i

over the velocity components a, with k_a their free parameters (sum_a k_a = k), I^a_i the normalized profile of a alone at pixel i and sigma_i the error of the normalized flux. R_a replaces the number of pixels N of AICc and BIC: the pixels a component influences, weighted by its depth and by the S/N. f = 1 is the analogue of AICc (SpIC_A), f = 0 of BIC (SpIC_B), f = 1/2 their hybrid (SpIC_H), which the paper finds the best all-rounder (section 5.3) and is spic here. R_a is bounded from below by k_a + 2, where the first term would diverge (section 2.4).

A velocity component of the paper is one redshift shared by several species (k_a = 6 for three species there): here, the components whose redshifts are linked by the constraints (z of one an expression of the z of another), one component alone when its redshift is not linked. Not the groups of the fits (SystemList.get_connected_group()), which also join the blended components.

astrocook.fitting.model_evaluation.SPIC_WEIGHTS = {'spic': 0.5, 'spic_a': 1.0, 'spic_b': 0.0}#

The weight f of the AICc-like term of the SpIC (Webb et al. 2021, eq. 3), for each SpIC reported: SpIC_H (spic, the one the paper recommends), SpIC_A and SpIC_B

astrocook.fitting.model_evaluation.MIN_INFLUENCE = 1.0#

The influence of a component on the pixels, sum_i ((1 - I_i) / sigma_i)^2, above which it is counted (its free parameters in k, its terms in the SpIC). For a weak, isolated component the sum is about the increase of the chi2 if it were removed: 1 means that the pixels see it at the level of 1 sigma. Without it, the components far from the pixels counted too (on a window of 5 nm of a mock with 140 components, k = 420 on 1286 pixels and a reduced chi2 of 1.47 for a chi2/n of 0.99); a rule on the centres of the lines missed the wings that do absorb in the pixels, so that a component at the edge of a window improved the chi2 for free (Guido, 16 November 2026: this criterion, threshold 1, variable)

astrocook.fitting.model_evaluation.OUTLIER_SIGMA = 3.0#

astrocook.fitting.model_evaluation.information_criteria(chi2: float, n: int, k: int) → Tuple[float, float | None, float | None][source]#

(AIC, AICc, BIC): AIC = chi2 + 2k, AICc = AIC + 2k(k+1)/(n-k-1) (None if n - k - 1 <= 0) and BIC = chi2 + k ln n (None without pixels); chi2 stands for -2 ln L, whose constant does not matter between models on the same pixels (as in metals_auto).

astrocook.fitting.model_evaluation.spic(chi2: float, terms: Sequence[VelocityComponent], f: float) → float[source]#

The SpIC (Webb et al. 2021, eq. 3) for the weight f; R_a bounded from below by k_a + 2 (section 2.4), a velocity component with no free parameter adds nothing.

astrocook.fitting.model_evaluation.evaluate_model(spec: Spectrum, systs: SystemList, mask: ndarray, uuids: Sequence[str] | None = None, min_influence: float = 1.0) → ModelEvaluation[source]#

Measure the model of systs (all its components) on the pixels mask (those without a valid flux or error left out), counting the components of uuids (None: all) whose influence on the pixels is at least min_influence (0: all of them; see MIN_INFLUENCE).

Raises:

ValueError – If no valid pixel is in mask.
