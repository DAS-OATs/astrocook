astrocook.fitting.component_flags module#

The flags of the components whose values are not measured (phase 7, PR 7d.2; findings register F52, F116).

The fit leaves on each component it fits the flags of #517 (a parameter at a bound, nan_errors: voigt_fitter.COMPONENT_FLAGS); this module adds those that say a value is there but is not a measurement:

  • unresolved: b is not measured, the line is narrower than the resolution;

  • saturated: log N is not measured, the weakest line of the series in the fit is saturated;

  • large_errors: the errors have no meaning (F52);

  • telluric_overlap: a line of the series falls on a telluric absorption, so its errors are not to be trusted.

They describe the last fit, as those of #517, and are annotations only: no number of the fit and no decision of a pipeline depends on them (design: ROADMAP.md, phase 7, “The flags of 7d.2”, agreed with Guido on 26 November 2026). measurement_flags() is a pure function of the component, the atomic data, the resolution and the telluric column, called by the fitter on the components it fits.

The thresholds are heuristics; what supports each is written next to it, and the measurement on the mocks is in the PR of 7d.2.

astrocook.fitting.component_flags.MEASUREMENT_FLAGS = ('unresolved', 'saturated', 'large_errors', 'telluric_overlap')#

The flags of this module, in the order they are added to ComponentData.flags (after those of #517)

astrocook.fitting.component_flags.FWHM_OVER_B = np.float64(1.6651092223153954)#

the b of the instrumental profile of resolving power R is c / (1.665 R) (4.0 km/s at R = 45000)

Type:

FWHM / b of a Gaussian profile, 2 sqrt(ln 2)

astrocook.fitting.component_flags.UNRESOLVED_RATIO = 0.5#

b below this fraction of the instrumental b. A line with b < b_inst / 2 has an observed width within 12 per cent of the instrumental one (sqrt(1 + 1/4) = 1.12), so its b is set by a few per cent of the observed width, below what a fit at the usual S/N resolves (F116: b = 1.4-2.8 km/s at R = 45000, b_inst = 4.0 km/s, judged unreliable by both agents of the trials of PR 7 of phase 6-ter)

Type:

unresolved

astrocook.fitting.component_flags.SATURATED_TAU = 3.0#

the central optical depth of the weakest line of the series above this. tau_0 = 3 is a transmission of 5 per cent at the centre of the intrinsic profile: from there the core is flat and log N moves the equivalent width only through the wings (the flat part of the curve of growth)

Type:

saturated

astrocook.fitting.component_flags.DAMPED_LOGN = 19.0#

its Lorentzian wings constrain log N

Type:

A damped H I (log N at or above this) is not flagged saturated

astrocook.fitting.component_flags.LARGE_DLOGN = 1.0#

the error of log N above this (dex), the rule of the benchmark since phase 4 (_flags of validate_mocks.py: a factor of 10 in N is no measurement). The other two: db > b, and the error of z in velocity above max(b, b_inst), the width of the line

Type:

large_errors (F52)

astrocook.fitting.component_flags.TELLURIC_DEPTH = 0.05#

the telluric depth 1 - T above this within the reach of a line. 5 per cent is about the noise of a pixel at S/N 20, the lowest of the real spectra of the benchmark: a shallower telluric line is below the noise of every spectrum of the sample (the threshold of the plan of 7d.2; its counts on the mocks with tellurics are in the PR)

Type:

telluric_overlap

astrocook.fitting.component_flags.TELLURIC_REACH = 2.0#

the reach of a line, in units of max(b, b_inst) around its centre: two Doppler widths hold the core of a Gaussian line down to exp(-4), 2 per cent of its central depth

Type:

telluric_overlap

astrocook.fitting.component_flags.large_error_params(comp: ComponentData, b_inst: float = 0.0) → Tuple[str, ...][source]#

The parameters whose errors have no meaning (F52): 'logN' if dlogN > 1, 'b' if db > b, 'z' if the error of z in velocity exceeds max(b, b_inst). Empty unless the three errors are finite (otherwise the flag is nan_errors).

astrocook.fitting.component_flags.measurement_flags(comp: ComponentData, pix: FitPixels) → Tuple[str, ...][source]#

The flags of MEASUREMENT_FLAGS of a fitted component, in their order: unresolved (b < 0.5 b_inst, R at the strongest line of the series), saturated (the central optical depth of the weakest line of the series in the fit above 3, on the intrinsic profile; not for a damped H I), large_errors (large_error_params()), telluric_overlap (a line of the series in the fit where the telluric column has 1 - T > 0.05 within 2 max(b, b_inst) of its centre; never without the column).
