astrocook.core.line_detection module#

Detection of the absorption lines by matched filters (phase 7, PR 7c.4b).

The direction of F57 and of section 5 of design/identification.md: the lines are searched on the flux normalised by the continuum with a bank of filters matched to the expected line widths, instead of the pixels that the clipping of the continuum rejects (abs_mask, which stays the mask of the continuum).

For each pixel and each width the significance of a Gaussian absorption centred there is

S = sum_i w_i (1 - F_i) / sigma_i^2 / sqrt(sum_i w_i^2 / sigma_i^2),

with w the filter, F the normalised flux and sigma its error: the amplitude of the filter fitted to the data by least squares, over its error. On pure noise S is a standard normal variable at each pixel (correlated between neighbours). The filter is a Gaussian in velocity whose variance is that of the instrumental profile plus that of a line of Doppler parameter b, b^2 / 2 (an optically thin line); three widths by default, b = 5, 15, 40 km/s.

A line is a pixel where S > LINE_SIGMA for any width; its region the run of pixels around it where S > EXTENT_SIGMA for any width (a hysteresis threshold). The multiplets are also searched on their joint significance at their lags: for the strongest line (highest f lambda) at a pixel, the filters of the other lines are scaled by their f lambda over that of the strongest (the ratio of their optical depths for a thin line) and placed at their lags,

J = sum_k r_k N_k / sqrt(sum_k r_k^2 D_k),

with N and D the numerator and the squared denominator of S of each line. A multiplet where J > MULTIPLET_SIGMA, each of its lines above EXTENT_SIGMA alone and the lines consistent with the ratio (multiplet_seeds()) gives a line at each of its lines, also where no line reaches the threshold alone (a doublet at 3 and 1.5 sigma has J of about 3.35); their regions are found as those of the other lines. At the default threshold of 4 sigma a doublet of thin lines (r of about 0.5) needs its stronger line above about 3.6 sigma, already a line alone: the joint search then gives its region to the weaker line, below the threshold alone, rather than finding doublets whose lines are both below it (that takes a threshold of J near 3, at about three times the false regions of the lines alone on pure noise: PR 7c.4b).

Not used by the pipelines yet (PR 7c.4c will): no recipe changes its numbers.

astrocook.core.line_detection.multiplet_seeds(joint: ndarray, s_lines: ndarray, line_sigma: float, extent_sigma: float) → ndarray[source]#

The pixels of the strongest line of a multiplet found by its joint significance (one filter): J > line_sigma, every line above extent_sigma alone, and the lines consistent with the ratio of their f lambda, chi2 = sum S_k^2 - J^2 <= CONSISTENCY_CHI2 (the residuals of the amplitude fitted to all the lines; one degree of freedom less than the lines).

The last two conditions keep a strong line from making a multiplet with the noise at its lags (a ghost at each lag of each multiplet around each strong line: on the mocks of the benchmark the whole forest and a quarter of the red side were in a region without them). A saturated multiplet, whose ratio is not that of f lambda, fails the consistency, but its lines are found alone.

  • z_max (float, optional) – No multiplet with its strongest line beyond this redshift (e.g. z_em and the margin of the associated absorbers, F55).
