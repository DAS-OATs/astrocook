astrocook.fitting.hypotheses module#

Identification as a comparison between hypotheses (phase 7, PR 7c.4c).

The prototype of design/identification.md (sections 3, 5, 6, 10-12), selected by identify_lines(method="hypotheses") next to the current method ("doublets", identify_lines()):

  1. Regions: the lines found by the matched filters of PR 7c.4b (detect_lines()), not the pixels that the clipping of the continuum rejects; each region is split into its peaks (the de-blending of the current method, on the normalised flux).

  2. Edges: two regions that a transition pair of a requested species puts at the same redshift (the anchors of ANCHOR_PAIRS: the doublets, two pairs of FeII and of SiII, and Ly-a/Ly-b in the forest), kept when the R^2 of F_1 = F_2^gamma (the score of the current method, now a cheap pre-filter) is above prefilter. The metals only on the red side of the Ly-a emission (the metals of the forest off: decision 2 of the design), the Ly-a/Ly-b pairs only in the forest, no system beyond z_em by more than the margin of F55.

  3. Groups: the connected components of the graph; a group of more than max_group regions is split at its weakest edges (lowest R^2).

  4. Hypotheses: per group, each edge (a candidate) as the anchor of one hypothesis, the other peaks of the group with their default label, and the hypothesis “all default”: n + 1 for n candidates. The default label is the Ly-a assumed in the forest when the Ly-a is asked (Ly_a or Ly_ab among the species, F51), the unidentified line otherwise: one free line per peak, the Voigt profile of a placeholder transition (the Ly-a at its wavelength, k = 3). Two candidates that win alone against “all default” and share no region are combined in one hypothesis.

  5. Evaluation: on an EvaluationWindow of the group (PR 7c.4a), the same pixels for all its hypotheses, every component counted (min_influence = 0). First with N and b from the apparent optical depth (AOD); the hypotheses behind the best by more than screen are dropped; the others (the finalists) get a short fit (FIT_NFEV evaluations) and are evaluated again.

  6. Credibility: w_i = exp(-dIC_i / 2) normalised over the finalists of the group, IC the SpIC_H by default (decision 1 of the design), BIC and the others reported; optionally tempered by the chi2 per degree of freedom of the accepted model (temper, section 3.3: off by default).

The result has the same form as that of the current method, so that the rest of the chain works unchanged: the abs_ids column (here every region, the unidentified ones too), the maps of the candidate systems (one per component of the accepted hypotheses that is not an unidentified line; their score the weight of the hypothesis, 0 for the assumed Ly-a as in the current method) with the parameters of the short fit as their guesses, the labels of the regions, the dropped candidates, and the structure of the identification (core/identification.py, PR 7c.3) with every group, its edges and all its hypotheses.

The higher orders of the Lyman series are not used (design, section 12); the assumed Ly-a has no Ly-b in its model (a Ly-b is modelled only by a Ly-a/Ly-b hypothesis, anchored by its edge); the tellurics (7c.5), the prior of the incidence and the sweep of the anchored systems (7c.4d) are not in the prototype.

astrocook.fitting.hypotheses.PREFILTER_R2 = 0.3#

the R^2 of F_1 = F_2^gamma of the pair (the score of the current method) at or above which a candidate becomes an edge of the graph. Its purpose: in the forest, and on the red side with the false regions of the bias of the continuum (F168), every region is at the lag of some other; without a pre-filter the groups percolate, every coincidence costs a fit and many win by chance. Measured in PR 7c.4c (18 mocks of the grid full, no screen): at 0 the purity of CIV and MgII fell to 0.56 and 0.52 (ECE 0.20, 36 s per spectrum, 10 minutes at R 140000); at 0.2 and 0.3 0.96 and 0.91-0.94, with the same completeness (ECE 0.02- 0.03, 4 s). 0.3 (chosen with Guido’s leave, 19 November 2026)

Type:

The pre-filter of the edges

astrocook.fitting.hypotheses.SCREEN_DELTA: float | None = None#

the hypotheses behind the best by more than this (in the criterion, evaluated with the AOD parameters) are dropped before the short fit; None: no screen, every hypothesis fitted. Its purpose: the time (a fit per hypothesis). Measured in PR 7c.4c at 10, 20 and 40: the AOD model of a strong line, close in logN and b to the fit (MgII logN 13.42, b 6.4 against 13.50, 8.0), has a chi2 of thousands at S/N 100 (7645 against 88), so every threshold of a few tens dropped about half of the true CIV and SiIV systems (completeness 0.44 against 0.77, ECE 0.31 against 0.03) for 2.5 s per spectrum saved. None (chosen with Guido’s leave, 19 November 2026)

Type:

The screen

astrocook.fitting.hypotheses.MAX_GROUP = 6#

above it the group is split at its weakest edges, so that the n + 1 hypotheses stay few and the windows short

Type:

The largest group, in regions (decision 5 of the design)

astrocook.fitting.hypotheses.CRITERIA = ('spic', 'bic', 'aic')#

SpIC_H (default), BIC, AIC

Type:

The criteria of the weights

astrocook.fitting.hypotheses.FIT_NFEV = 300#

The short fit of the finalists (the experiment of the design, section 4: 300 evaluations; the bounds those of the experiment, wider in b than the 50 km/s of the pipelines, as the unidentified lines can be blends)

astrocook.fitting.hypotheses.MAX_FIT_K = 60#

The largest hypothesis that gets the short fit, in free parameters (20 components of three). Its purpose: the time. The Jacobian of the fit is computed by finite differences, one model per parameter, so a fit costs about k times FIT_NFEV models; a group with a wide region (the trough of a DLA, mock “combined” of the grid robustness at S/N 10) had hypotheses of 94 components, k = 281, and one mock did not end in 100 minutes. A group with a hypothesis above it is not compared: its default labels are accepted with their AOD parameters, as a group without edges (60: Guido, 19 November 2026)

astrocook.fitting.hypotheses.MAX_ADDED = 3#

after its short fit a hypothesis gets a component where the residuals of its model are most negative (a 3-pixel sum below -REFINE_SIGMA): one of its species when the pixel is in a region of one of its anchors, an unidentified line (or the assumed Ly-a) elsewhere, kept when it lowers the criterion; at most MAX_ADDED. Its purpose: the components of a hypothesis are the peaks of the de-blending, fewer than the true ones in a saturated or blended system; with too few, a metal hypothesis cannot fit both lines with one ratio, and the free lines of “all default” win on the misfit (on the mocks of the grid the CIV, SiIV, MgII systems of 3-4 components lost by 20-60 in the SpIC). Every finalist gets it, so that each identification is compared with its best model of a few more components

Type:

The refinement of the finalists by their residuals

astrocook.fitting.hypotheses.PLACEHOLDER = 'Ly_a'#

astrocook.fitting.hypotheses.parse_species(multiplets: Sequence[str]) → Tuple[List[str], bool][source]#

The anchors and whether the Ly-a is asked, from the species of the recipe: "all" is every metal anchor; Ly_ab asks the Ly-a and its pairs with the Ly-b, Ly_a the Ly-a alone (the assumed Ly-a of the forest).

astrocook.fitting.hypotheses.pair_score(v: ndarray, flux: ndarray, region_strong: Tuple[int, int], region_partner: Tuple[int, int], lines: Tuple[str, str], z: float) → float[source]#

The R^2 of F_s = F_p^gamma of a pair (the score of the current method, rate_doublet_candidate(), for any two transitions): the absorption of the strong line against that of the partner raised to gamma = (f lambda)_s / (f lambda)_p, on the velocities where the two regions overlap at the lag, 30 km/s more on each side, in the frame c ln(lambda / lambda_s). 0 when it cannot be rated.

astrocook.fitting.hypotheses.anchor_peaks(c: _Candidate, by_id: Mapping[int, _Region], v: ndarray, fwhm: ndarray) → List[Tuple[int, int]][source]#

The peaks of the region of the strong line of a candidate whose partner line falls on a peak of the region of the partner (pairs of their indices): where the pair is a doublet. None for a candidate whose regions overlap at the lag but whose peaks do not: its pair says nothing of the species.

astrocook.fitting.hypotheses.aod_guess(v: ndarray, flux: ndarray, err: ndarray, lines: Sequence[str], z: float, aod_range: Tuple[float, float], fwhm_kms: float) → Tuple[float, float][source]#

logN and b of a component from the apparent optical depth of its lines within aod_range (km/s from each line): N the largest over the lines (the weaker one corrects the saturation of the stronger), b from the FWHM of the apparent optical depth of that line, the instrumental one (fwhm_kms) subtracted in quadrature (a second moment of N(v) over the range of a blend took the noise of its wings; the width of the flux of a saturated line was too wide). Within AOD_LOGN and AOD_B.

astrocook.fitting.hypotheses.identify_hypotheses(spec: Spectrum, multiplets: Sequence[str], fixed: SystemList | None = None, criterion: str = 'spic', prefilter: float = 0.3, screen: float | None = None, max_group: int = 6, temper: bool = False, z_em_margin_kms: float = 3000.0, x_range: Tuple[float, float] | None = None, params: Mapping[str, Any] | None = None) → HypothesesResult[source]#

The identification as a comparison between hypotheses (see the module) of the regions of spec.

Parameters:

  • spec (Spectrum) – With a continuum, the resolution and z_em.

  • multiplets (sequence of str) – The species searched (parse_species()).

  • fixed (SystemList, optional) – Components that stay fixed in every evaluation (those already in the session).

  • criterion (str) – "spic" (SpIC_H), "bic" or "aic": the weights.

  • prefilter (float) – The R^2 of a pair above which it is an edge (PREFILTER_R2).

  • screen (float or None) – The screen of the AOD stage (SCREEN_DELTA); None for none (every hypothesis fitted).

  • max_group (int) – The largest group, in regions (MAX_GROUP).

  • temper (bool) – The differences of the criterion divided by max(1, chi2_nu) of the accepted model of the group (design, section 3.3).

  • z_em_margin_kms (float) – No system beyond z_em by more than this (F55).

  • x_range ((float, float), optional) – Only the regions whose centre is in this range of wavelength (nm).

  • params (mapping, optional) – The parameters of the recipe, for the structure.

Raises:

ValueError – Without z_em, a continuum or the resolution, for an unknown criterion or species.
