astrocook.fitting.evaluation_window module#

The evaluation of alternative models in a window of the spectrum (phase 7, PR 7c.4a; design/identification.md, sections 3.3 and 10).

The identification compares the hypotheses of a group of absorbed regions on the same pixels: the union of the windows of +-(3 FWHM + 5 b) around every line of every hypothesis of the group, and of the pixels that a fit of each hypothesis takes from the data (out to the continuum or to a saddle between blended lines), so that a broad line started with a small b is compared on its whole profile, not on its core (Guido, 18 November 2026). evaluate_model() and the short fit of VoigtFitter compute the model and the profiles on the whole spectrum, so their cost follows the length of the spectrum, not the pixels of the comparison (about 1 s per hypothesis on a mock of 85 nm, PR 7c.2).

An EvaluationWindow cuts those pixels out of the spectrum once per group, as a spectrum of their own, and selects once the components that stay fixed (the rest of the spectrum, outside the group) whose lines reach them: both are shared by every hypothesis of the group. The evaluation and the short fit of a hypothesis then run on the window, with its components and those fixed ones (frozen, not counted), through the same functions as on the whole spectrum; within a fit the optical depth of the fixed components is computed once (the background of VoigtFitter.prepare_fit_context()).

The short fit is not limited to the window: it takes its pixels from the data as on the whole spectrum (VoigtFitter.feature_mask(), out to the continuum or to a saddle between blended lines), cut out of the parent per fit. Limited to the window, which follows the b of the start, a broad or saturated line started narrow was fitted on its core alone (logN 16, b 60 came out as 15.1, b 80 at R 45000) and a line in a crowded region, with the fixed list imperfect, moved by more than 0.01 dex or 1 km/s in 10-23% of the fits (Guido’s concern, PR 7c.4a); with the pixels of the data the fit is that on the whole spectrum.

The statistics agree with those on the whole spectrum to about 1e-5 of the chi2 (the fine grid of the model follows the narrowest line in the pixels, which on the whole spectrum can be another one). Folding the fixed components into the continuum instead, as their transmission convolved apart and multiplied, was measured and dropped (PR 7c.4a): it was not faster, and where a fixed line is blended with a line of the group it moved the difference of the criteria between two hypotheses by 0.2 at R 45000 and 1.3 at R 8900 (exp(-tau_1 - tau_2) convolved is not the product of the two convolved).

astrocook.fitting.evaluation_window.WINDOW_FWHM = 3.0#

The half-width of the window around a line, in units of the instrumental FWHM and of the b of its component: +-(3 FWHM + 5 b) (decided with Guido, 18 November 2026, design/identification.md section 10): the wings of the line convolved with the resolution are below the noise beyond it, and the pixels of the continuum on both sides are part of the comparison (a hypothesis that puts absorption where there is none pays for it there)

astrocook.fitting.evaluation_window.FIT_PAD = 60#

Pixels added on both sides of the pixels a fit takes from the data, in the spectrum cut for the fit: the fitter computes its model on its pixels and 50 more on each side (prepare_fit_context) and smooths the flux over about 8 when it looks for the limits of a line; with 60 the fit on the cut is that on the whole spectrum

astrocook.fitting.evaluation_window.window_mask(spec: Spectrum, hypotheses: Sequence[SystemList], fwhm_factor: float = 3.0, b_factor: float = 5.0) → ndarray[source]#

The pixels within +-(fwhm_factor FWHM + b_factor b) of a line of a component of any of hypotheses: the FWHM the instrumental one at the line (c / R of the nearest pixel; 0 where the resolution is not known), b that of its component (with btur).

The window of the comparison of hypotheses, the same pixels for all of them: window_mask() (+-(3 FWHM + 5 b) around their lines) and the pixels that a fit of each of them takes from the data (VoigtFitter.feature_mask(), out to the continuum or to a saddle between blended lines, whatever the b of the start; with z_window_kms as in the fit), with fixed. Without the second, a broad line started narrow was compared on its core alone (Guido, 18 November 2026).

The pixels of the parent on which fit() fits systs: the window, and the pixels that a fit of its components takes from the data on the whole spectrum (VoigtFitter.feature_mask(), out to the continuum or to a saddle between blended lines, whatever the b of the start), each run widened by FIT_PAD pixels.

fit(systs: SystemList, **kwargs: Any) → SystemList[source]#

The fit of the components of systs (a hypothesis) by VoigtFitter.fit() with kwargs (e.g. the max_nfev, b_max and logN_max of a short fit), the fixed components that reach its pixels frozen. Its pixels are those of fit_pixels(): the fit is that on the whole spectrum, not limited to the window (a broad or saturated line started with a small b, or a line in a crowded region, takes its pixels from the data, PR 7c.4a). Returns systs with its components fitted (a list without components as it is).
