astrocook.recipes.prerequisites module#

What a recipe needs from the session, and who provides it (phase 6-bis, PR 5).

The registry declares what each recipe requires (z_em, the resolution, the continuum) and what it provides; this module says what is missing for a recipe (missing(), for the GUI and the MCP server, which ask before the recipe runs) and fills the gaps at the boundary of the recipe (fill(), called by typed_recipe), following a policy chosen by the script:

  • "provide" (the default): what a recipe can provide with its defaults is provided first, with a WARNING (the continuum, by Auto-estimate Continuum); what no recipe can provide by itself (the emission redshift, the resolution) is left to the recipe, which goes on as before with its own WARNING (Guido: the numbers do not change);

  • "raise": a MissingPrerequisite that says what is missing and which recipe provides it.

The policy is set for a block of a script with policy(), or for one call with run(..., on_missing="raise").

astrocook.recipes.prerequisites.POLICIES = ('provide', 'raise')#
