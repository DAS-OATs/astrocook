astrocook.core.identification module#

The identification of the absorbed regions as a structure (phase 7, PR 7c.3).

The requirements are in design/identification.md, section 9: per group of regions, the regions and the edges that made it, the hypotheses that explain it (their components, stage, statistics, weight, status and reason), the candidates dropped by the score, and the parameters of the run. The structure is immutable (frozen dataclasses, tuples) and lives in the metadata of the spectrum under META_KEY, next to the abs_ids column that it describes, so that it follows the history of the session and the undo with no extra code. In the archive it is a file of its own, <name>_identification.json (astrocook.io.archive_data): a FITS header cannot hold it.

RegionKind is the vocabulary of the regions shared with the emission model of 7b (design/identification.md, “Common decisions with 7b”, item 1): 7b writes the regions it owns (BAL troughs, DLA cores and wings, tellurics), the identification labels the others.

The current method of identify_lines() (method="doublets") fills the structure in a minimal form: every region it finds, the accepted pairs as edges, one hypothesis per accepted pair (stage Stage.SCORE, the R^2 of the pair as its only statistic) and one per region of the forest assumed to be Ly-a, the dropped candidates.
